This is a personal side project. The CU coefficients it uses are my own rough estimates, not published Microsoft figures — they are all visible and editable under Assumptions at the bottom of the tool, and you should replace them with numbers measured in the Fabric Capacity Metrics app for your own tenant.

Do not use this to quote a customer, size a purchase, or make a budget decision. Treat it as a way to compare shapes and find roughly where a SKU boundary sits — nothing more. Pricing and platform behaviour both drift; verify against Azure pricing before relying on any number you see here.

Which capacity does a Fabric App actually need?

Four groups of plain questions → an expected CU consumption, the smallest capacity that survives your peak, and what that costs per month. Most small apps land further down the SKU list than people expect — a typical one peaks around 0.35 CU. Nothing is sent anywhere; the whole thing runs in your browser and makes no network calls.
